# Week 1 KPI Proposal & Analysis Questions

## Proposed KPIs
1. **Inventory turnover** — how fast stock sells, by product and branch
2. **Stockout rate** — share of products below reorder level at a point in time
3. **Supplier on-time delivery rate** — purchase orders received by expected date vs total
4. **Revenue and margin by product** — from sales order lines
5. **Warehouse space utilisation** — stock volume vs warehouse capacity, by branch
6. **Invoice payment status mix** — share of invoices Paid / Partially Paid / Unpaid
7. **Order cancellation rate** — cancelled vs total, for both sales and purchase orders

## Analysis questions for the team
1. Which products are fast-moving vs slow-moving/dead stock?
2. Which branches are over or under capacity, and does that match their sales volume?
3. Which suppliers are most reliable, and which pose dependency risk?
4. Which customers are high-value vs at risk of churn?
5. What is driving the unpaid/partially paid invoices — specific customers, branches, or time periods?

## Notes
These are a starting proposal from Week 1 profiling. We will refine them in Weeks 2–3 once the dataset is fully cleaned and validated.
